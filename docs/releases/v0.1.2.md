A new calendar, built for busy teams: readable when a week holds hundreds of tasks, and fast at any size.
No database migrations and no configuration changes; just run the new image.

## A calendar that stays readable

- **Grouped busy slots.** When more than three tasks overlap, or a day has more than three daily tasks,
  week and day views show one block per project (per environment when you filter to a single project),
  at most three side by side, with "+N projects" for the rest. Quiet slots still show tasks one by one.
  A **Group busy slots** switch turns it off (on by default, remembered per browser).
- **Side panel.** Click a group or "+N more" to list its tasks with dates, key, environment, project and
  status, and open any of them.
- **Project filter.** Pick any set of projects (or "Independent tasks") from a searchable list with
  checkboxes and per-project task counts.

## Our own calendar views

LajurOps no longer uses FullCalendar; month, week, day and agenda are built into the app and match the
rest of the UI.

- **Week and day:** a 24-hour grid that opens at 07:00 with a now-line, multi-day bars in the all-day row,
  and overlapping tasks side by side. Click or drag over empty time to create an hourly task with that
  range; click the all-day row to create a daily task.
- **Month:** six weeks with multi-day bars, timed tasks as dot and time, and "+N more" per day. Click a
  day to create a daily task, or its date to open that day.
- **Agenda:** day by day, all-day work first, with environment, project and status.
- The view you use is remembered.

Tasks are no longer dragged on the calendar: open a task to change its dates. (Dragging still works on the
boards and the timeline.)
