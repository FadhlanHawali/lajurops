A new **Weekly Commitment** page: each person says what they'll finish this week, and the team sees everyone's plan.
One database migration (011, adds two tables) runs automatically on start; no configuration changes.

## Weekly Commitment

- **My week.** Pick the daily tasks you'll finish from three tabs: **Overdue**, **Assigned to you** and
  **Unassigned** (picking one assigns it to you). Drag or click +, reorder, set your capacity, add a note for
  the team and save. A picked task without dates is scheduled Monday to Friday of that week.
- **Hourly tasks are automatic.** An hourly task assigned to you that starts in the week is part of your
  commitment; reschedule or reassign it to change that. Planned hours count hourly time the same way as
  Hourly Workload (overlapping tasks once) plus the daily tasks' estimates, and turn red over capacity.
- **Overdue.** Tasks you committed to in the last 4 weeks that aren't done show as a red badge on the sidebar
  and the My week tab, a one-line notice with **Review**, and an Overdue tab grouped by week (with dates).
  Re-pick daily ones one by one or with **Add all daily**; open hourly ones to reschedule.
- **Team.** One card per person: daily and hourly done counts, hours, *Over capacity*, *Daily not picked*
  and *N overdue* chips, each task's project and environment, and the note. Filter by workspace.
- **Tick tasks done** (or reopen them) right from both views.
- **Week picker** with week numbers, plus Last / This / Next week.

## Also

- **Workload is now Hourly Workload**, and says plainly that its hours come from hourly tasks only.
- **To be arranged.** Daily and hourly tasks have a button to clear their dates until someone plans them.
- `GET /api/tasks?assignee_id=none` lists unassigned tasks. New endpoints: `GET /api/commitments`,
  `GET` / `PUT /api/commitments/me` (see the README).

## Fast with big teams

Measured with 300 people and 111,000 tasks:

| Weekly Commitment | Time |
|-------------------|------|
| My week | **33 ms** |
| Save a commitment | **88 ms** |
| Team, one workspace | **65 ms** |
| Team, all 300 people | **0.27 s** server, **0.65 s** on screen (433 KB download) |
| Open a tab with 1,800 tasks | **41 ms** |
