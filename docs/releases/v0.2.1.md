Share work on Weekly Commitment: commit to a task someone else owns and join them on it.
No database migrations and no configuration changes; just run the new image.

## Assigned to others

- **A new tab on My week.** Next to *Assigned to you* and *Unassigned*, **Assigned to others** lists other
  people's open daily tasks for the week (or without dates) in workspaces you can edit, with their owners'
  avatars. It loads when you open it.
- **Join as an owner.** Pick one with + or by dragging; it shows *you'll be added as an owner* until you
  save. Saving adds you next to the existing owners (nobody is removed) and, like any pick, schedules it
  Monday to Friday of that week if it has no dates.
- `GET /api/tasks?assignee_id=others` lists tasks owned by other people and not by you.
